Global Planetary Analog Discovery — Flow B Architecture

Flow B (flow_B_global_discovery) extends the selection engine of Flow A from a closed catalog of known terrestrial sites to an automated global planetary discovery system. While Flow A evaluates analogs already documented in scientific literature, Flow B scans the entire emerged Earth surface using Google Earth Engine (GEE), geographically excludes Flow A sites, extracts multi-sensor telemetry from NASA and ESA orbital missions, and projects abiotic cells into the latent space calibrated on Flow A’s MATLAB core to discover novel lunar and Martian analogs[cite: 5].

1. Module Architecture: Inputs & Outputs

The pipeline is structured into four active Python modules (alongside the initial STAC prototype archived in legacy/):

  • perseus_common.py
    Role: Shared utilities, Haversine geodetic distance calculations, and loading calibration anchors.
    Inputs: data/Earth_Lunar_Analogs_Catalog.csv, data/Earth_Mars_Analogs_Catalog.csv.
    Outputs: Deduplicated Flow A anchor DataFrame and minimum distance vectors dmin (km).
  • gee_global_extract.py
    Role: Server-side extractor on Google Earth Engine across two stages, plus physical and geotechnical derivation.
    Inputs: Global equal-area grid (0.25°), NASA/ESA/ECMWF satellite collections, Flow A anchors.
    Outputs: cache/global_abiotic_grid.csv, cache/extraction_metadata.json.
  • run_flow_B_discovery.py
    Role: Mathematical engine for standardization, PCA/SVD projection, K-Means clustering, and multi-criteria scoring.
    Inputs: cache/global_abiotic_grid.csv, target catalogs Lunar_Targets_Catalog.csv (5 targets) and Mars_Targets_Catalog.csv (10 targets).
    Outputs: results/flowB_{lunar,mars}_heatmap.json, results/flowB_{lunar,mars}_discoveries.geojson, results/flowB_exclusion_report.json, results/flowB_*_feature_parity_report.csv.
  • generate_local_viewer.py
    Role: Standalone cartographic web-app generator for local visualization and GitHub Pages deployment.
    Inputs: Heatmap and exclusion JSON files in results/.
    Outputs: results/perseus_local_viewer.html (deployed as discovery_map.html)[cite: 5].

2. NASA & Orbital Partners Data Processing

Radiometric and topographic processing executes server-side on Google Earth Engine datacenters over a multi-seasonal climatological window of 4 years (2021–2024). Raw orbital imagery is transformed into 28 physical and engineering variables across four processing chains:

  • A. Multi-Scale Topography & Morphometry (NASA NASADEM + Copernicus GLO-30 / CryoSat-2)
    Sources: NASA/NASADEM_HGT/001 (30 m native resolution, derived from SRTM) fused seamlessly with COPERNICUS/DEM/GLO30 and CPOM/CryoSat2/ANTARCTICA_DEM to cover polar latitudes beyond ±60°.
    Geometric Treatment: To eliminate longitudinal distortion near the poles (Δx ∝ cos φ), neighborhood statistics are computed over a metric circular kernel (ee.Kernel.circle(radius=win_px * 30.0, units="meters"), 480 m radius, area ≈ 0.72 km²).
    Derived Quantities: Elevation (Altitude_m), local slope at 30 m (Slope_30m_deg), smoothed slopes at 90 m and 270 m, mean landing slope (Mean_Slope_deg), 75th-percentile rover traversability (Traverse_Slope_deg), safe landing fraction (Safe_Landing_Frac, slope ≤ 5°), ultra-flat fraction (Ultra_Flat_Frac, ≤ 2°), and root-mean-square surface roughness (RMS_Roughness_m).
  • B. Optical Spectroscopy & Mineralogy (NASA/USGS Landsat 8 & 9 Collection 2 Level-2)
    Sources: LANDSAT/LC08/C02/T1_L2 and LANDSAT/LC09/C02/T1_L2 (plus T2_L2 collections for Antarctic orbits) at 30 m resolution.
    Cloud Masking & Radiometric Calibration: Bit-masking on QA_PIXEL (qa.bitwiseAnd(30).eq(0)) removes clouds, cloud shadows, cirrus, and snow/ice. Digital Numbers (DN) across six bands (SR_B2–SR_B7) are converted to physical surface reflectance (ρλ = DNλ · 0.0000275 − 0.2) and aggregated via multi-year median.
    Geochemical & Spectral Proxies: Normalized difference indices (NDVI, NDWI, NDSI), broadband albedo (Albedo), ferric oxide index (Ferric_Oxide_Idx), mafic/basaltic index (Mafic_Basalt_Idx), FeO and TiO2 weight percentages, salts and evaporites (Salts_Evaporites_wt_pct), and water ice (Water_Ice_wt_pct) constrained thermodynamically by Tmin < 273.15 K.
  • C. Thermophysics & Thermal Inertia (NASA MODIS Aqua MYD11A2)
    Source: MODIS/061/MYD11A2 (8-day composites at 1 km resolution, 13:30 and 01:30 local solar time).
    Thermal Treatment: Scaled to Kelvin (× 0.02) to derive mean temperature, 5th percentile (Tmin), 95th percentile (Tmax), and clear-sky fraction (Sun_Visibility_pct).
    Empirical Thermal Inertia Calibration: Apparent Thermal Inertia ATI = (1 − A) / ΔT is computed from diurnal temperature amplitude ΔT and albedo, then calibrated against Flow A anchors via median scaling factor kTI to yield TI = kTI · ATI.
  • D. Atmosphere & Soil Geotechnics (ECMWF ERA5-Land + OpenLandMap)
    Atmosphere (ECMWF/ERA5_LAND/MONTHLY_AGGR): Mean and peak monthly 10 m wind speeds, surface pressure (Surface_Pressure_mbar), solar UV flux proxy (UV_Flux_Index), and volumetric soil moisture (swvl1).
    Regolith Mechanics (OpenLandMap): Granulometric fractions (sand_pct, clay_pct) and bulk density yield internal friction angle φ, cohesion c, Terzaghi bearing capacity (Bearing_Capacity_kPa), and eolian deflation index (Eolian_Deflation_Idx)[cite: 5].

3. Four-Level Cascade Filtering Pipeline

  1. Barrier 1 — Equal-Area Grid & Flow A Geographic Exclusion (50 km): build_grid() generates a global equal-area sampling grid (Δφ = 0.25°, ≈ 27.8 km spacing). min_distance_to_sites_km() removes every cell within 50 km of known Flow A sites (dmin ≥ 50.0 km).
  2. Barrier 2 — Server-Side Coarse Mask (Stage 1 at 1 km): Queries MODIS/061/MCD12Q1 (IGBP land cover) and MODIS/061/MOD13A3 (monthly NDVI). Eliminates forests, wetlands, croplands, urban centers, water bodies (LC_EXCLUDE), and vegetated regions with NDVImax ≥ 0.20.
  3. Barrier 3 — Strict Abiotic Filter & Telemetry Integrity (Stage 2 at 30 m): Parallelized via ThreadPoolExecutor with incremental disk checkpoints. Enforces a strict abiotic threshold of NDVILandsat < 0.15 (while preserving Flow A calibration anchors with is_known_site=True).
  4. Barrier 4 — Lander & Rover Engineering Constraints (run_flow_B_discovery.py): Evaluates operational feasibility flags: Lander_Suitable (Mean_Slope_deg ≤ 5.0° and Safe_Landing_Frac ≥ 0.50) and Rover_Suitable (Traverse_Slope_deg ≤ 12.0°)[cite: 5].

4. Classification & Scoring in Latent Space

  • Feature Intersection & Cross-Domain Alignment: build_calibration_space() isolates 13 shared numerical features across terrestrial catalogs, planetary targets, and satellite grids, aligning physical scales via median ratios derived from Flow A anchors.
  • Dual-Mode Standardization: Applies standard Z-score normalization for the Moon (lunar) and Domain-Adapted Z-Score for Mars (mars), standardizing terrestrial cells and Martian targets against their respective planetary distributions. Z-scores are clipped to [−4.5σ, +4.5σ].
  • SVD/PCA Projection & K-Means Clustering: Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) on the Flow A reference matrix (Zref) retains ~95% cumulative variance (6 principal components for the Moon, 7 for Mars). Global candidate cells are projected via Sg = ZgV, and a K-Means (K = 3) model assigns geological archetypes (Cluster_ID and Cluster_Label).
  • Hybrid Similarity Index: Euclidean distance in PCA space d = ‖Sg − st‖2 is mapped via exponential decay: Scorelatent = 100 · exp(−ln(2) · d / dmedian,FlowA), then fused (75% weight) with the 4-axis Radar Chart sub-score mean (25% weight)[cite: 5].

5. Exported Artifacts & Web Visualization

  • JSON Heatmaps & Top-25 Rankings: flowB_{lunar,mars}_heatmap.json and flowB_{lunar,mars}_all_targets_top25.json covering all 5 lunar and 10 Martian targets.
  • GeoJSON Discoveries: Standard RFC 7946 vector layers (flowB_{lunar,mars}_discoveries.geojson) ready for QGIS and ArcGIS integration.
  • Audit Reports: flowB_exclusion_report.json and feature parity CSV reports verifying cross-domain consistency.
  • Interactive Web Viewer: perseus_local_viewer.html (discovery_map.html), a standalone Leaflet satellite map featuring planet/target selectors, 50 km exclusion rings, engineering suitability badges, and full telemetry popups[cite: 5].
